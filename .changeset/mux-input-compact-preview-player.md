The video and audio cards in the Videos tool now play with compact controls (play/pause, the current time, which switches to the remaining time on click, a thin timeline that shows video chapters, with a hover preview of the thumbnail, chapter title and time, and mute with a volume slider on hover) so the controls no longer cover most of the preview or crowd the timeline. Playback errors show over the card until dismissed. The asset details dialog and the input field keep the full player. The play button shown over thumbnails before playback now uses the Video.js icon and control styling. Audio cards keep the same height as video cards and show the audio file icon in the middle of the card, and clicking an audio card plays or pauses it like a video card.
