Run Mux Robots workflows from the Studio: open Robots from a video's menu or from its tab in the Videos tool to run any of the twelve workflows, follow and cancel jobs, and read their outputs. Every job on the video and the newest summarize and moderate outputs are recorded on the `mux.videoAsset` document. Only administrators can run Robots by default; set `allowedRolesForRobots` to choose the roles. Chapters tracks are now listed apart from captions and no longer count as captions.
